The idea of my portal is basically connecting students or freelancers to businesses for projects or job opportunities. I want to allow seamless search for businesses to find talents to either make their ideas come true or even a long time employee. This portal allows users to create a custom portfolio to post their past projects, experiences, videos, about them, etc. They even have tailored Resume + Cover letter for the job they are applying. Theres even a linkedin content generator to expose themselves to other Linkedin users. Its an all in 1 package utilizing AI generation.

A platform where users can generate interesting names for their business. It can be from Playful like Google to Real Words like Amazon and Freeform with the help of AI. After generating the names, the agent will search if the TLDs are available.
